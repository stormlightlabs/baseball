Known gaps & caveats
Retrosheet pre-1918: Event-level data is sparse or absent for most seasons before 1918. Game-level summaries may still exist via Lahman.
Negro Leagues: Lahman and Retrosheet have limited coverage of Negro League seasons. Data should not be used for cross-era comparisons without noting this gap.
Federal League: Dedicated coverage exists only for 1914-1915 via /v1/federalleague/*. Treat cross-league comparisons as historical context, not 1:1 modern equivalence.
1994 strike season: Lahman has partial season records. Some aggregate statistics may be incomplete.
Fielding data: Pre-1920 fielding records have known errors and missing fields in Lahman. Use with caution.
Live data lag: MLB StatsAPI data may lag real-time by up to 15 minutes during games. Historical backfill is applied within 24 hours of game completion.

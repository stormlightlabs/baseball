Negro Leagues Era nlg
The Negro Leagues operated from the early 1900s with peak coverage here from 1935–1949. Multiple circuits ran concurrently — primarily the Negro American League (NAL) and Negro National League (NNL). Data is sourced from Retrosheet with known gaps.

## FanGraphs Guts Constants

wOBA weights, league wOBA, wOBA scale, FIP constants, and park factors are taken from FanGraphs' Guts tool. wOBA definitions follow Tom Tango's formulation as documented in the FanGraphs Library.

- [FanGraphs Guts Tool](https://www.fangraphs.com/tools/guts)
